Helping More Pets Find Homes Through Data

Brulee Pawlytics is a pet-focused analytics consulting firm specializing in adoption event optimization for animal shelters.

What We Do

Brulee Pawlytics is a data analytics consulting firm focused on helping animal shelters make data-driven decisions that improve adoption outcomes. We analyze adoption trends, visitor behavior, event performance, and operational metrics to identify strategies that help more animals find permanent homes.

Our work focuses on transforming shelter data into actionable insights. Whether evaluating adoption open houses, examining seasonal adoption patterns, or measuring the effectiveness of outreach efforts, our goal is to support organizations with evidence-based recommendations.

How We Do It

We combine statistical analysis, data visualization, and predictive modeling to better understand the factors that influence successful adoptions. By examining historical shelter records and event data, we identify trends that may not be immediately visible through traditional reporting methods.

  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
  • Data Visualization and Reporting
  • Predictive Modeling
  • Adoption Event Evaluation
  • Operational Performance Analysis

Our approach emphasizes clear communication and practical recommendations so that analytical findings can be translated into real-world improvements.

Who We Serve

  • Animal Shelters
  • Pet Rescue Organizations
  • Animal Welfare Nonprofits
  • Veterinary Clinics
  • Community Adoption Programs

By partnering with organizations dedicated to animal welfare, we aim to use data as a tool for improving outcomes, increasing adoption success, and helping more pets find loving homes.
