Helping Animal Shelters Turn Data Into More Adoptions

Brulee Pawlytics is a pet-focused analytics consulting firm that helps animal shelters and rescue organizations use data to improve adoption events, understand visitor behavior, and connect more pets with loving families.

Many animal welfare organizations collect valuable information through adoption records, community events, visitor interactions, and outreach campaigns. However, they may not always have the time, tools, or analytical support to turn that information into clear decisions. Brulee Pawlytics bridges that gap by transforming shelter data into practical insights.

What We Do

We help shelters make data-driven decisions that improve adoption outcomes. Our work focuses on analyzing adoption trends, event performance, visitor engagement, and operational metrics to identify strategies that help more animals find permanent homes.

Instead of only reporting numbers, we translate data into recommendations that shelters can actually use. Our goal is to help organizations plan stronger adoption events, understand what works, and improve the experience for both pets and potential adopters.

How We Help

  • Analyze adoption trends and seasonal patterns
  • Evaluate adoption event performance
  • Study visitor behavior and engagement
  • Measure the effectiveness of outreach campaigns
  • Create clear data visualizations and reports
  • Develop practical recommendations for shelter operations

Our approach combines statistical analysis, data visualization, and clear communication. We focus on making analytical findings easy to understand so that shelters can use them to make real-world improvements.

Who We Serve

  • Animal Shelters
  • Pet Rescue Organizations
  • Animal Welfare Nonprofits
  • Veterinary Clinics
  • Community Adoption Programs

By partnering with organizations dedicated to animal welfare, Brulee Pawlytics aims to use data as a tool for improving adoption success, supporting shelter operations, and helping more pets find loving homes.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to help animal welfare organizations make smarter decisions with data. Every chart, report, and recommendation we create is designed to support one larger goal: helping more pets find safe, caring, and permanent homes.
